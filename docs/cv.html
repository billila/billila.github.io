PhD Thesis

Development of efficient and scalable methods for omic data analyses in cancer biology

The thesis addresses the computational challenges posed by large-scale single-cell and digital pathology data. It benchmarks state-of-the-art PCA/SVD algorithms and GPU-accelerated pipelines for single-cell datasets with millions of cells, and develops workflows to extract and integrate nuclei-level and slide-level features from over 11,000 TCGA histopathology images, releasing dedicated R/Bioconductor tools and a Shiny application.
